# Duntong Image-Gen Prompts

Concept visualization only; not fabrication authority. Do not infer tongue
lengths, shell thickness, port size, head hardware, or tuning from these
prompts.

## Hero Prompt

Photorealistic 3/4 product photograph of the Duntong, Tony Koop's original cylindrical tongue-drum concept: a medium stave-built round wooden cylinder with a single ring of eight tuned tongue shapes cut around the equator, warm Padauk, cherry, or maple wood, smooth turned ends, and an optional sealed end, ported end, or goatskin drumhead variant visible at one side. The instrument should read as a dundun-inspired body crossed with a wooden tongue drum, playable from multiple sides, with soft mallets nearby for scale. Clean studio background, soft directional lighting, 50mm lens, high detail, photorealistic.

## In-Context Prompt

Photorealistic workshop scene of the Duntong prototype being tested on a bench cradle: a player uses soft mallets to strike the tongue ring around the cylinder while another end configuration, wrap template, tuner, and CNC V-block cradle sit nearby. Natural shop light, visible stave construction, believable hand reach around the shell, and an exploratory original-instrument mood.

## Detail / Macro Prompt

Photorealistic macro close-up of the Duntong tongue band: curved-shell wooden cantilever tongues cut cleanly around a cylindrical stave body, fine slit kerfs, visible equator layout marks removed or faint, and a smooth satin finish showing grain direction across each tongue. Show the shell curvature and tongue relief clearly, with a soft mallet head entering the edge of frame. High-detail macro, shallow depth of field.

## Negative / Avoid

No text, logos, watermarks, flat box tongue drum, impossible curved slits, fake note labels, extra limbs, distorted hands, or cutaway dimensions.
